Before you arrive

Healing sessions with Saha

Healing sessions are optional. If you plan to book one, read this first and watch the short videos.

Reconnective Healing

A non-touch modality working with energy, information and light to bring body, mind and spirit into balance. It can be done in person or remotely, and most people need one session, three at most. You don't need any technology: simply lie down at the agreed time.

  • Video: introduction to Reconnective Healing
  • Video: a second overview
  • Article: Reconnective Healing, The Reconnection

Quantum Healing Hypnosis (QHHT)

A deep hypnosis method that guides you into the theta brainwave state for insight, healing and clarity. A QHHT session is in person only, needs a six-hour window of undisturbed time, and the rest of the day should be kept free to rest and integrate. Prepare your questions in advance.

Question ideas to prepare for QHHT

Health & body

  • Why am I experiencing this health condition?
  • What is the root cause of this condition?
  • What is this condition showing me?
  • What does my body need from me right now?
  • What is the optimal diet for me?
  • What supplements, if any, support me best?
  • What supports my optimal health and immunity?
  • What form of movement or exercise suits me best?
  • Are any medications I take unnecessary for me?
  • Why do I feel tired?
  • What is the cause of pain or discomfort in my ______?
  • What in my body needs attention, care, or healing?
  • What is required for this to fully heal?

Emotional & inner healing

  • Is there unresolved trauma in my body?
  • What inner child wounds are ready to be healed?
  • What is the root cause of my anxiety, depression, or emotional patterns?
  • How can I release anger, guilt, grief, or resentment?
  • What fears am I holding onto?
  • What is the origin of these fears or phobias?
  • In what ways is my body communicating with me?

Self-awareness & integration

  • What parts of myself have I ignored or rejected?
  • What aspects of myself are ready to be integrated?
  • What patterns or blocks am I holding?
  • Why do I repeat the same patterns?
  • What limiting beliefs do I hold?
  • What rituals or practices support me now?
  • What is most important for me to know at this time?

Purpose & life path

  • What did I come here to do?
  • Am I aligned with my life's purpose?
  • What lessons am I here to learn?
  • What conditioning or programming do I need to release?
  • Where am I being guided to live or move?

Relationships & family

  • How can I improve my relationship with ______?
  • What limiting beliefs do I have about relationships?
  • What is the deeper reason for challenges with ______?
  • How can I be a more present parent?
  • What does my child need me to understand?
  • How can I be a more conscious partner?
  • What role do I play in my relationships?
  • What boundaries serve me best?
  • Is there anyone I need to forgive?

Ancestral & soul-level

  • What is my role in my ancestral healing?
  • Are there patterns I carry from my lineage?
  • Are there agreements or contracts I need to release or honor?

Career, money & expression

  • Am I aligned with my current work?
  • What path or career suits me best?
  • Why do I struggle financially?
  • What blocks my abundance?
  • What ideas am I meant to act on?

Spiritual connection & awareness

  • How can I deepen my connection with my Higher Self?
  • What is the best meditation practice for me?
  • How can I better receive guidance?
  • What is the meaning of my recurring dreams?
  • Why do I feel like I don't belong here?
  • What abilities or sensitivities are ready to open?
  • What is the meaning behind signs or synchronicities I experience?

Open inquiry

  • What does my animal companion want me to know?
  • What is the meaning behind a strange or inexplicable experience I had?
  • What is the significance of ______?
  • Are there any limiting beliefs I have about sexuality and intimacy?
  • Is there a healing modality I should learn or practice now?
